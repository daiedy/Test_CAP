# Glossary

The words are in alphabetical order. A word in bold inside an entry has its own entry.

## A

**Active product**: the saved version of a product that everyone sees in the list. It changes only when a **draft** is saved. See [Edit, save, keep or discard a draft](how-to/Edit-save-keep-or-discard-a-draft.md).

**Attribute**: one kind of fact about a product, such as Colour or Weight. The attributes offered for a product depend on its **subcategory**. A product holds one **detail** per attribute. See [Detail attributes](domain/Detail-attributes.md) and [Fill the details of a product](how-to/Fill-the-details-of-a-product.md).

## C

**Category**: the broad group a product belongs to: Accessories, Electronics, Furniture, Kitchen, Sports or Stationery. Every product has exactly one. See [Categories](domain/Categories.md) and [Choose a category and subcategory](how-to/Choose-a-category-and-subcategory.md).

**CatalogEditor**: the role that may create, change, delete and import products. See [Who may do what](how-to/Who-may-do-what.md).

**CatalogViewer**: the role that may only read the catalog. See [Who may do what](how-to/Who-may-do-what.md).

**Currency**: the money the price of a product is given in, written as a three-letter code such as USD.

## D

**Detail**: one value of one **attribute** of one product, for example the attribute Colour with the value Black. The values are typed: text, whole number, decimal number, one choice from a list or several choices from a list. See [Product details](domain/Product-details.md), [Detail value types](domain/Detail-value-types.md), [Detail list values](domain/Detail-list-values.md) and [Product detail selections](domain/Product-detail-selections.md).

**Draft**: your private working copy of a product while you create or change it. Nobody else sees it, and it does not change the **active product** until you save. See [Create a product through a draft](how-to/Create-a-product-through-a-draft.md).

## L

**List Report**: the first screen of the catalog application: a filter bar and a table of products.

## O

**Object Page**: the screen of one product, with its sections General Information, Pricing and Stock, Details and Administrative Data.

**OData**: the standard way the catalog exposes its data to other programs. See the [API](api/API.md).

## P

**Product**: the one thing the catalog holds. It has a name, a price in a currency, a stock, a **category**, a **subcategory**, optional rating, description and image address, and its **details**. See [Products](domain/Products.md).

## R

**Rating**: a whole number from 0 to 5 that tells how customers rate the product. It is optional.

**Rule**: a statement about what the catalog accepts or does, with a stable id such as `BR-PRODUCTS-001`. See [Business rules](rules/Business-rules.md).

## S

**Subcategory**: the narrower group inside a **category**, for example Laptops, Mice and Audio inside Electronics. A product has exactly one, and it must belong to the product's category. See [The subcategory belongs to the product's category](rules/The-subcategory-belongs-to-the-products-category.md).

## V

**Value type**: how an **attribute** is filled: text, whole number, decimal number, one choice from a list, or several choices from a list.
