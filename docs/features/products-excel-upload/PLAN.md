## Risks
- Declarative checks may not fire on a service-internal `INSERT` (undocumented); detection: research 5.3 in step 5 before any error mapping is written; reaction: stop and revise ADR-0021, not an imperative copy.
- A `LargeBinary` inside a structured action parameter may arrive in an unexpected shape or as `Edm.Stream` that FE sends differently; detection: research 5.1, 5.2 and 6.3.
- Fiori Elements may not render a file field for a collection-bound action parameter on UI5 1.152 (documented example is instance-bound); detection: research 6.2 in step 8; reaction: the fallback gate of step 8. Resolved: 6.2 rendered the field.
- The required marker and the empty-submit check rest on UI5 1.152 sources, not on a fiori-mcp document (research 7.2); detection: step 8c; reaction: the stop of step 8c, never a controller extension without a user gate.
- `@mandatory` on `file` may change the server's answer to a request without `file` (a framework `ASSERT_MANDATORY` instead of the handler's workbook error); detection: research 6.5 in step 8a; both are 400 and the dialog never sends such a request; the test of step 8b asserts the measured `code`.
- The empty-file text is a framework text of the unpinned CDN UI5 and may be reworded; OPA5 asserts the value state and the label inside the text, not the sentence.
- The absolute `Common.SideEffects` path goes through the entity container like the `$Path` of the known `$select` TypeError; detection: step 8c and `ui-verifier` console; accepted as known noise if identical in shape.
- The `$batch` body of a large file may exceed the 100 KB body-parser limit (413); detection: research 5.6 and the 1,000-row test; reaction: the conditional service annotation of step 6.
- Per-row inserts for 1,000 rows may be slow; detection: research 5.7; above 10 s on SQLite in-memory the architect reconsiders the insert strategy before phase 3.
- A second instance of the known `Permissions/isEditor` `$select` TypeError; detection: `ui-verifier` console section; accepted as known noise if identical in shape.
- `UI.Hidden` driven by the singleton may resolve differently from `UI.CreateHidden` (asymmetric failure, CHANGELOG 2026-09-23); detection: research 6.1 and `ui-verifier` as `viewer` and `alice`.
- A test that imports rows leaves them in the in-memory database and shifts later row counts in `test/catalog-service.test.js`; detection: `npm test`; `test-backend` deletes the imported rows at the end of the `describe` or asserts relative counts.
- OPA5 cannot choose a file in the browser's file dialog; the upload itself is covered by the backend tests and `ui-verifier`, OPA5 covers the button, the dialog and Cancel only.
